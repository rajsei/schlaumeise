Keyboard shortcuts let you execute commands faster and edit text or files more efficiently. Most combinations work similarly across many programs and are also useful in the console.

## Copy

- **Windows/Linux:**
  - Press <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">C</kbd>

- **macOS:**
  - <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">C</kbd>

## Paste

- **Windows/Linux:**
  - Press <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">V</kbd>

- **macOS:**
  - <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">V</kbd>

Note: In some Linux terminals, pasting works with <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">V</kbd>.

## Cut

- **Windows/Linux:**
  - Press <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">X</kbd>

- **macOS:**
  - <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">X</kbd> or <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd>+<kbd class="kbd">X</kbd>
