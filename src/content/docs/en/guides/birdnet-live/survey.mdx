## Typical use cases

Survey Mode is especially suited for:

- walks and hikes with continuous recording
- surveys along fixed routes
- transect mapping
- surveys where the location of each detection matters
- citizen-science projects with a spatial reference

## How Survey Mode works
<NumberedList>
1. Open **Survey Mode**.
2. Complete the **setup wizard**.
    - Enter the **details**. For the start location, it's advisable to use the current GPS position.
    - Set the **parameters**. See [Settings](../settings/) for more information. The default settings are suitable for most applications.
    - Configure **species alerts**.
      The app can alert you to certain species during the survey. For this feature, we recommend using headphones. That way the voice output is not picked up again by the microphone and does not affect the analysis.
      The announcements are especially helpful when you carry your smartphone in your pocket during a hike.
    - Review the **field tips**.
    - Check the configuration and **start the survey**.
3. **Monitor the live survey.** During the survey, the dashboard shows:
    - a **route map with detections**
    - an **audio spectrogram**
    - an **overview with a species breakdown**
    - a list of **possible species in your area**
4. **Let the survey run in the background.**
5. **End the survey and evaluate it**

    In the live dashboard, tap the red button to **end the survey**.
    The app saves the finished survey.
    BirdNET Live then automatically opens the **Session Review**.
</NumberedList>
<br />

<div role="alert" class="alert alert-warning ">
  <span>For survey mode to record your location correctly, BirdNET Live needs permission to access your location in the background as well.
To do this, open your **smartphone's settings** and go to **Apps → BirdNET Live → Permissions → Location**.
Then select **"Allow all the time" (Android)** or enable **continuous location access (iOS)**.
Only with this permission can BirdNET Live reliably record your route during a survey recording
– even when the app is in the background or the display is turned off.</span>
</div>

## Evaluating the results

After you end the survey, BirdNET Live automatically opens the **Session Review**.
There you can see your route with all located detections on the map, listen to individual detections, view spectrograms, and add metadata.

Later, you'll find the survey at any time in **Sessions**, where you can export the results or share them with others.
