The ARU Mode turns your smartphone into an autonomous recording device (Autonomous Recording Unit, or ARU for short).
Instead of running continuously, BirdNET Live starts recordings automatically on a defined schedule – for example at sunrise or sunset – making it ideal for long-term surveys at a fixed location.

During an ARU session, the app records in freely configurable recording and pause intervals.
The recorded audio data is analyzed directly on the smartphone.
To save storage space, you can optionally keep only the best detections.
This makes the mode especially suitable for long-term observations and monitoring projects.

## Typical use cases

The ARU Mode is especially suited for:

- long-term recordings at a fixed location
- continuous surveys in the early morning or at dusk
- monitoring in nature conservation projects
- surveys spanning several days or weeks
- unattended use in the field

## How the ARU Mode works
1. Select the ARU Mode in BirdNET Live.
2. Check the **parameters**.
For most hobby applications, the default settings can be kept unchanged.
They are already optimized for typical long-term recordings.
3. Set a **schedule**. Define when recordings should start and end automatically. This lets you specifically capture the morning or evening hours, for example.
4. **Position your smartphone** in a suitable place.
    - Protect the device from rain and moisture, e.g. with a resealable freezer bag or a junction box from the hardware store.
    - Place the smartphone as freely as possible and away from sources of interference such as roads, running water, or strong wind.
    - Point the microphone toward the recording area as much as possible.
    - Prepare the smartphone for longer recordings:
      - For longer recordings, connect a power bank or charge the smartphone regularly.
      - Make sure there is enough free storage space.
      - Enable airplane mode to save battery, and additionally turn on power-saving mode.
5. **Start the session**. BirdNET Live then handles the recordings automatically according to your schedule and analyzes them directly on the device.
