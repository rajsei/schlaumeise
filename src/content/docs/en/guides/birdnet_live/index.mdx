Turn your smartphone into a powerful tool for automatic bird song recognition. With BirdNET Live, you can identify bird species in real time based on their songs and calls—without any additional hardware.

The app uses the latest BirdNET+ AI model and analyzes ambient sounds directly on your device. All calculations are performed locally on your smartphone or tablet. Your audio recordings do not need to be uploaded to the cloud, so your data remains on your device at all times. Once the required models are installed, BirdNET Live works completely offline.

BirdNET Live was developed as a joint open-source project by the Cornell K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation Bioacoustics and Chemnitz University of Technology. The app is designed for bird enthusiasts, citizen scientists, conservation projects, and researchers who want to record bird songs reliably and in a privacy-friendly manner.

Depending on your use case, various modes are available:



- Live Mode – Identify bird calls in real time and watch as new identifications appear while you listen.
- Point Count – Conduct time-limited surveys at a fixed location—ideal for standardized mapping.
- Survey Mode – Document longer walks or transects with GPS support and continuous recording.
- File Analysis – Analyze existing audio recordings directly on your device.
- Explore – Find out which bird species are expected in your area and during the current season.
- Session Library – Manage past recordings, review identifications, and export your results in various formats.
