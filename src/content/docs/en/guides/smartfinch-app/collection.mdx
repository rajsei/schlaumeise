Two tiles on the home screen answer two questions:
the **Collection** shows what you already have. **Explore** shows what you could still find.

## The Collection

The Collection holds **every animal you have found** – with a photo, its name and its value.
It is what you browse through in bed in the evening: who do you have already? How many is it by now?

At first the Collection is empty. Every bird you hear ends up here.
An animal stays in your Collection even when it isn't around right now – the swallow in December just as much as a bird you found on holiday.

### All or this year

At the top you can switch between two views:

- **All** – every animal you have ever found.
- **This year** – only the animals you have found this calendar year.

"This year" starts again from zero every 1 January. Nothing is lost: under "All" your Collection stays complete.
That way every year becomes exciting again, and you see who comes back in spring.

### What do the cards show?

- **"Here, this week: 200 ⭐"** – this is how many stars the animal is worth where you are right now, if you hear it for the first time today.
- **"Not here this week"** – the animal doesn't usually occur where you are this week, for example a migrant in winter.

Above the Collection it says how many species there are in your region, for example **"37 of 128 species in your region"**.
Explore shows you which ones you are still missing.

### Filters

With the filters you can sort the Collection by group: **birds**, **mammals**, **amphibians** and **insects**.

If you have ever heard something that was **not counted** – for example in test mode, without a location, or an animal that isn't expected here – an extra **"Not counted"** filter appears.
When you switch it on, you see only those animals, with a grey photo and the note "Heard, not counted".
They are not part of your Collection. If you find one of them properly later on, it is still a first find and earns ×3.

## Explore

Explore shows you **which animals occur where you are at this time of year** – the most likely first.
The list is calculated by the BirdNET AI's geo model directly on your phone.
A **tick** means: you have already collected that one.

So you could, for example, set yourself a goal: "This week I want to find a great spotted woodpecker."

<NumberedList>

1. **On the home screen, open the "Explore" tile.**
2. **Look at the list.**

    The circle on each card fills up and turns from red to green the more likely the animal is where you are right now.
3. **Filter and search.**

    Choose a group (birds, mammals, amphibians, insects), show only species you have already detected or not yet detected, or search for a name.
4. **Tap an animal.**

    You see pictures, a description, links to more information and the **year curve**.

</NumberedList>

### The year curve

For every animal, the year curve shows **when in the year it occurs where you are**.
For a blackbird it is high all year round. For a barn swallow it rises in spring and falls again in autumn.
It also shows you why an animal is sometimes worth more stars and sometimes fewer.
