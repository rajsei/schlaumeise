Stars are the currency of Smartfinch.
Every animal you hear earns you stars – and the rarer it is where you are, the more you get.
All the rules are on this page. You can also find them in the app: under **Help → How do I earn stars?**

## Rarer animals are worth more

Every animal has a **level** where you are. A sparrow you hear every day is worth a little.
An animal that almost nobody hears here is worth a lot.

| Level | Stars |
|---|---:|
| Everywhere | 50 ⭐ |
| Common | 100 ⭐ |
| Now and then | 200 ⭐ |
| Not so often | 350 ⭐ |
| Rare | 600 ⭐ |
| Very rare | 1,000 ⭐ |

The app works out the levels **for your location and the current week**.
To do so, it compares all the animals that occur where you are: the commonest land in "Everywhere", the rarest in "Very rare".
That is why there are 1,000-star animals to discover everywhere – on a city balcony just as much as at the edge of a wood.

You can look up an animal's value in advance: in the Collection and in Explore, each species shows, for example, **"Here, this week: 200 ⭐"**.

## Each animal once a day

The **first time** you hear an animal on a given day, you get its stars.
If you hear it again on the same day, the card just says **"already collected today ✓"**.
The next day, you get the full stars again.

This is the most important rule in the whole game. Two hours at the same window are worth exactly as much as ten minutes.
Ten minutes at the edge of a wood or by a pond, on the other hand, can bring completely new animals.
**Going somewhere else is worth more than standing still.**

A day runs from midnight to midnight, a week from Monday to Sunday.

## Sometimes it counts double or triple

Some finds are worth more than usual. The card then shows a bonus label:

| Bonus | When? | Stars |
|---|---|:---:|
| **First find** (`NEW`) | You hear the species for the very first time. | ×3 |
| **First this year** (`FIRST THIS YEAR`) | You hear the species for the first time this year. | ×2 |
| **Regular** (`REGULAR`) | You hear the species on the 3rd day in one week. | ×2 |
| **Always here** (`ALWAYS HERE`) | You hear the species on the 6th day in one week. | ×3 |

**Only the best bonus counts** – they don't add up.
A first find that is also the first this year therefore earns ×3, not ×6.
That is why a single animal can never earn more than 3,000 stars in a day.

Because every species is "first this year" again on 1 January, every spring becomes exciting again:
the first blackcap of the year earns double stars, even if you have already heard it three times before.

## Extra stars for a good day

The more different animals you hear in a day, the more extra stars you get:

| Different species in one day | Extra stars |
|---|---:|
| 5 | +50 ⭐ |
| 10 | +150 ⭐ |
| 15 | +300 ⭐ |
| 20 | +500 ⭐ |
| 25 | +750 ⭐ |

The extra stars add up: with 15 species you have collected 50 + 150 + 300 = **500 extra stars**.

There is also the **early riser bonus**: if you hear your first animal before 9 in the morning, you get **+50 ⭐** once a day. Every way to school counts!

Extra stars are never doubled or tripled. They are only added after the bonus for the animal has been worked out.

## Examples

This is how the stars add up (the levels in the examples are made up – where you are, they may be different):

| Find | Calculation | Stars |
|---|---|---:|
| Blackbird, second time today | – | 0 ⭐ |
| Blackbird ("Everywhere"), first time today | 50 × 1 | 50 ⭐ |
| Robin ("Common"), first find ever | 100 × 3 | 300 ⭐ |
| Great tit ("Everywhere"), on the 3rd day this week | 50 × 2 | 100 ⭐ |
| Great tit ("Everywhere"), on the 6th day this week | 50 × 3 | 150 ⭐ |
| Blackcap in May ("Common"), first time this year | 100 × 2 | 200 ⭐ |
| Blackcap in January ("Rare"), first time this year | 600 × 2 | 1,200 ⭐ |

In the journal, every find shows how many stars it earned and which bonus applied.
**Every number can be checked.**

## Why do the points change during the year?

Because the birds change. Many fly away in autumn and come back in spring.
In May a blackcap is one of many – in January there is hardly one here. Hearing one then is a real find.
Same bird, same rule: **it is worth what it is worth here right now.**

Birds that stay all year, such as the blackbird or the great tit, hardly change their value.
Migrants, on the other hand, can jump one or two levels depending on the season.
That way you learn along the way who arrives when, who leaves when and who stays.

Important: stars you have earned **stay as they are**.
A find from May is not suddenly worth more or less in December.

## And why somewhere else is different

The levels are worked out for your location. A gull is nothing special by the sea, but it is something inland.
That is why everything is worth something different on holiday. That is not a mistake – it is exactly what makes going somewhere else exciting.

## When are there no stars?

Sometimes the app recognizes an animal but awards no stars. There is always a reason, and the app tells you which one:

- **You have already collected the animal today.** Tomorrow there are stars again.
- **The animal is not expected where you are this week.** It then has no level here – and without a level there are no stars. This way the AI's most likely mistakes are not rewarded.
- **The app has no location.** Without a location it doesn't know which animals live here.
- **Test mode is on**, because the species filter has been switched off or the confidence threshold lowered below 35 %.

What you hear in such cases is not lost: it appears in the journal as **"outside scoring"**, and in the Collection you can show it with the **"Not counted"** filter.
If you find such an animal properly later on, it is still a first find and earns ×3.

## Nothing is ever taken away

There are **no penalties** in Smartfinch. Stars are never deducted, and your levels never go down.
Even if you remove a misidentified bird from the journal, you keep the stars for it.
