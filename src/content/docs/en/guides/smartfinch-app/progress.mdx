Smartfinch should be fun even when no bird is singing – in bed in the evening, for example.
That is what the points, the badges, your own bird and the stickers are for.

## Your stars on the home screen

At the top of the home screen you see your stars at a glance:

- **all the stars** you have ever collected,
- the stars of the **last 30 days**,
- today's stars, for example "Today: ⭐ 350",
- and seven small bars for the last seven days. The last bar is **today**.

## The "Points" area

The **Points** tile takes you to your statistics. They have three tabs: **Overview**, **Badges** and **Achievements**.

### Overview

A bar chart shows your **stars per day** for the last 7 days, 30 days or a whole year.
Tap a bar to open that day in the journal.

Below it are your most important figures:

- **Stars in total**
- **Species collected**
- **Days outside** – days on which you heard at least one animal that counted
- **Longest run** – the most days in a row on which you were outside

### Badges – for today and this week

You can earn badges again and again. You see all of them: the ones you have earned and the ones that are **still open** today or this week.

| Badge | When do you earn it? |
|---|---|
| 🌅 **The early bird** | Heard something before 9 in the morning |
| 🌆 **Evening listener** | Heard something after 6 in the evening |
| 🎵 **Dawn chorus** | 5 different species between 5 and 9 in the morning |
| 🔟 **Ten in one go** | 10 different species in one day |
| ✨ **Discovery day** | Found a species for the first time ever |
| 🥇 **Rare guest** | Heard one of the rarer birds around here |
| 🌱 **Herald of spring** | Heard a bird that is normally not here yet |
| 🗺️ **New ground** | Listened somewhere you had never listened before |
| 🌙 **Night owl** | Heard something after 10 at night |
| 📅 **Consistent** | Listened on 4 days of one week |
| 🌍 **Well travelled** | Listened in 3 different places in one week |
| 🎯 **Weekly target** | 5,000 stars in one week |

On top of that come your **regulars**: species you heard on 3 days of one week (**Regular**) or even on 6 days (**Always here**).

### Achievements – for good

You earn each achievement only once, and it stays forever. Here you only see the ones you have already earned.

**Species collected:**
10 species – *First steps* · 25 – *Attentive listener* · 50 – *Half a hundred* · 75 – *Connoisseur* · 100 – *The big hundred* · 150 – *Species hunter* · 200 – *Ears like a lynx* · 250 – *Ornithologist*

**Rare finds:**
*Lucky one* (1 of the rarer birds) · *Tracker* (5) · *Rarity collector* (10) · *Sensation!* (one of the very rarest birds)

**This year:**
*Year list I, II and III* (25, 50 and 75 species in one year) · *All year round* (something heard in every month of a year) · *The returners* (10 species first heard in spring) · *Winter visitors* (5 species in December or January)

**Staying power:**
*A week kept up* (7 days in a row) · *A month of bird ears* (30 days in a row) · *A hundred days outdoors* (100 days altogether) · *Star collector I, II and III* (10,000, 50,000 and 100,000 stars)

## Your bird

At the top of the home screen sits **your own bird**. Tap it to give it a name.
With your stars it climbs the **levels** and grows along the way: from an **egg** to a **chick** and a **fledgling** to a **grown bird**.

You start at **level 0, the egg**. The first levels come quickly – on the first evening you often reach two or three.
After that each level takes a little longer, but never too long. There is no end.

The levels come with titles that tell the story of a bird:

*Egg* → *Chick* → *Nestling* → *Fledgling* → *Young bird* → *Scout* → *Listener* → *Singer* → *Territory holder* → *Far flier* → *Migrant* → *Returner* → *Old bird* → *Flock leader* → *Legend*

It takes about three years to become a legend. And: **a level you have reached once, you never lose again.**

## Stickers

For **every new level** from level 1 on, you may pick a **sticker**: a hand-drawn bird from around the world.
Once picked, the sticker reveals a fact about the bird ("Did you know?"), which you can read again at any time.

<NumberedList>

1. **Open the sticker album** with the button under the level bar.
2. **Tap a bird you like** and take it.
3. **Decorate your home screen.**

    Pull the panel with the buttons down (if you hold the phone sideways: to the right). Now you can move the stickers with one finger and turn, enlarge or shrink them with two fingers.
4. **Push the panel back** when you are done. Everything is saved.

</NumberedList>

A sticker is **not a find**: it doesn't go into your Collection and doesn't change your stars.
