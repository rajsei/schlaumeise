In the journal, **every day you listen gets an entry**.
It is not sorted by individual recordings but by days – because you remember a day, not a recording.
When you stop listening, the journal opens by itself and shows you your day.

## Days, weeks, months and years

At the top you can choose how far you want to zoom out: **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**.
Weeks and months show you, for example, on how many days you were outside and how many new species (✨) you found.

Each day card shows the date, your stars, how many species you heard and a preview of the animals.
If you also heard something that day that wasn't counted, it says something like "3 more outside scoring" underneath.

## A day in detail

Tap a day to see everything you heard that day:

- **every species** with the stars it earned you and the bonus that applied,
- a **✨ NEW** on species you found for the very first time that day,
- the **bonuses for the day**, for example extra stars for lots of different species,
- and animals **outside scoring** – with the reason why they earned no stars.

Expand a species to see **when** you heard it and how sure the AI was.
Tap a find to **play the recording**. You also see its spectrogram.

### Keeping recordings

So that your phone doesn't fill up, the app gradually tidies away old recordings – starting with species you have heard very often.
You can mark a recording you particularly like with **"Keep"**. Kept recordings are never deleted to make room.

## Giving places a name

Where were you? Give the place where you listened a name of your own – for example **"Grandma"**, **"Holiday"**, **"Way to school"** or **"By the pond"**.
You can still do that in the evening. Names you have used before can be picked again with one tap.
That way you still know weeks later where you heard which bird.

Place names stay on your phone. They are never shared.

## Wrong bird?

The AI can be wrong. If you are sure a bird was misidentified, you can **take it back**:

<NumberedList>

1. **Open the day in the journal and expand the species.**
2. **Tap "Wrong bird?".**
3. **Choose what you want to remove.**

    Only this one find or all finds of this species from that day – mistakes often come several times in a row.
4. **Read carefully what will happen.**

    The app tells you which bird from which day will disappear. The **"Remove"** button can only be tapped after five seconds, so that you don't delete anything by accident. "Cancel" works straight away.

</NumberedList>

The bird then disappears from your journal and – if nothing else of it is left – from your Collection. Its recordings are deleted.
**You keep your stars.** At the very bottom of the day, the find is listed under **"Removed"**, together with the stars you keep.

If you really hear the bird later on, it is new to your Collection again and gets celebrated.
You have already received the ×3 bonus for the first find, though – it isn't paid twice.

## Deleting a recording

If you only want to get rid of a recording, tap **"Delete recording"** in the player.
Then only the sound recording disappears. The bird, its stars and its place in the Collection stay.

## Sharing your bird day

Want to show Grandma everything you heard today? Then tap **"Share this day"** in the day.
The app turns it into a picture, **"Your bird day"**, with the date, the stars and the species of the day.

The picture contains **no recordings, no location and no place names** – only what you see before you share.
This is the only way anything leaves the app for other people.
