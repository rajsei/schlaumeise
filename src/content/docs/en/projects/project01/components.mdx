## Checklist: What do I need?

<Checklist id="project01-components">

- A **Raspberry Pi**
  - **Raspberry Pi 5** approx. $50–140 (2–16 GB RAM)
  - **Raspberry Pi 4B** approx. $40–80 (1–8 GB RAM)
  - **Raspberry Pi 3B** approx. $40
  - **Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W** approx. $20 — the least powerful supported model. If you choose it, invest in a **high-performance SD card**, since a large part of the system load is shifted onto the card. Not all BirdNET-Pi functions run reliably on it; to avoid crashes, it is advisable to disable features such as live audio.
- **SD card** at least 8 GB
  - For the Raspberry Pi 5, 4B, and 3B+, a normal SD card is usually sufficient – for example a Class 10 or UHS-I card with good read and write speeds.
  - For the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W we recommend a high-quality SD card, since a larger part of the system load is offloaded onto it and the card's performance and durability matter more here.
- possibly an **adapter** for a microSD card to your computer
- **Power supply** for your Raspberry Pi
- **USB microphone** or **USB sound card and AUX microphone**
- For the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, a **microUSB-OTG adapter** is additionally required
- Optional, for setup without SSH or for troubleshooting:
  - **Mini-HDMI-to-HDMI adapter** to connect the Pi to a screen
  - **USB keyboard** – pay attention to which and how many USB ports your Pi provides

</Checklist>
