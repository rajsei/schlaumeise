Have you always been curious about what's singing on your balcony or in your garden?
The listening birdhouse makes it possible!
Inside it sits a small Raspberry Pi running BirdNET – an AI that recognizes bird songs.
The system listens around the clock and shows you which species are chirping in your area.

In this project, you build your own little bird station step by step.
First you set up the SD card and the Raspberry Pi, install BirdNET-Pi, and then place the system in a suitable spot.
After that, the station can record bird songs, evaluate them automatically, and display the detected species through a web interface.

The project combines crafting, nature observation, and digital technology:
out of just a few parts, you create a device that listens outdoors or at your window and helps you get to know the birdlife around you better.
You can use it in the garden, on the balcony, or in another sheltered place.

## What will you build?

In the end you'll have a working BirdNET-Pi station with:

- a Raspberry Pi as a small computer,
- a microphone for recording bird songs,
- BirdNET-Pi for automatically recognizing the species,
- a web interface where you can view your observations,
- an optional hotspot, in case the station will be placed outside your Wi-Fi range.

## Prior knowledge

Basic computer skills are required; a little English is helpful.
You can follow the guide step by step even without deep technical knowledge – but it will be easier if you already have experience with the console and basic programming.
If you have any questions, feel free to reach out by email.
