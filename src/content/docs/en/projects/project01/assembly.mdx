So how do I get the BirdNET-Pi out into the garden?

## Options

There are several ways to place the BirdNET-Pi outdoors:

### Birdhouse:
You can build the Pi into a wooden birdhouse. Keep in mind that it is not weatherproof.
So place it in a sheltered spot and bring it indoors when it rains or freezes.

### Junction box:
At DIY stores you can find junction boxes for electrical installations. These work well to protect the Pi from the weather.

### Running only the microphone outside:
You can also run just the microphone outside.
That way the Pi stays dry, and you can design the housing however you like.
