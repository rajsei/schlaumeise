For the station to hear as many bird songs as possible, the spot and the microphone matter.
The Raspberry Pi and power supply are not weatherproof. Outdoors, they need a sheltered spot or an enclosure.

## Finding the right spot

- **Close to the birds:** Hedges, trees, feeders or a quiet edge of the garden are good spots. The closer the birds sing, the more reliably BirdNET identifies them.
- **Little noise:** Roads, fans, air conditioners and running water drown out quiet bird songs.
- **Wind and rain:** Wind causes noise in the microphone. A spot sheltered from the wind and a foam or fur windscreen help. Point the microphone downward or to the side so no rain gets in.
- **Power:** The station needs permanent power. Outdoors, only use outlets and extension cords rated for outdoor use.
- **Shade:** In direct sun, a closed enclosure heats up quickly. You can see the Pi's temperature in the system status under `Settings` → `Device`.
- **Reachability:** If the station is outside your Wi-Fi range, you reach it through its hotspot. You need to be nearby for that; the range depends on walls and the Pi model.

## Options for the setup

The same setups that work for a BirdNET-Pi station also work for Smartfinch Pi:

- **Wooden birdhouse:** looks nice but is not weatherproof. Place it somewhere sheltered and bring it inside when it rains or freezes.
- **Junction box from the hardware store:** an inexpensive, weatherproof enclosure for the Pi and power supply.
- **Only the microphone outside:** The Pi stays dry indoors, for example on a windowsill, and only the microphone cable goes outside.

## Check after placing

<Steps>

1. Open the station's web interface. The `Overview` should not show a notice such as “No microphone found”.

2. Open `Settings` → `Device` and tap `Test level` in the `Microphone level test`.
   If the station reports `Level OK`, everything is ready.

3. Be patient: the first detections appear as soon as a bird sings nearby. Most birds are active at dawn.

</Steps>

## Switching off the station

Don't simply pull the plug. If the power is cut while data is being written, the SD card can be damaged.

<Steps>

1. Open `Settings` → `Device` → `Restart & shut down` and tap `Shut down`.

2. Wait until the green LED on the Pi stops blinking.

3. Now disconnect the power. To switch it on again, plug the power supply back in; the station starts and keeps listening by itself.

</Steps>
