You open the web interface in the browser at `http://<device-name>.local:8080`, or in hotspot mode at `http://10.42.0.1:8080`.
The menu leads to four pages: `Overview`, `Detections`, `Statistics` and `Settings`.
A preset can limit which of them are visible (see [below](#presets)).

## Overview

The start page shows what the station has heard today:

- today's number of detections and species, when a bird was last detected and which species were most frequent,
- the latest detections,
- a notice when something needs to be done, for example when bird detection is paused (`Start now`) or no microphone was found,
- the weather of the day, which you can also enter here. Weather can be switched off in the settings.

## Detections

Each detection appears as a card with:

- the name of the species and its scientific name,
- how confident BirdNET is: `Very likely` from 90 %, `Likely` from 75 %, below that `Possible`,
- the time,
- the audio recording to `Listen` to and a spectrogram that shows the pitch over time,
- a link `More about this species ↗`,
- more information under `Details`, where you can also delete the detection or just its recording.

The `All detections` page lists every detection so far, newest first.

Even a confident detection can be wrong. Listen to the recording if a species seems unusual to you.

## Statistics

The statistics show detections over a longer period:

- **Over time:** detections and species per day, week, month or year as bars and a table.
- **Activity by time of day:** for each species, a small curve over 24 hours with sunrise and sunset. This shows which species sing early in the morning and which in the evening.
- **Day heatmap:** one row per species and one column per hour for a chosen day.
- **Species:** all detected species with their counts. Tapping a species shows its detections.

If you have entered weather, you can filter the statistics by weather, for example to show only sunny days.

## Settings

The settings are divided into five sections:

| Section | What you will find there |
| --- | --- |
| `General` | Appearance (light/dark), language and formats, preset, weather, location, time & date |
| `Device` | System status (e.g. temperature), microphone level test, stored recordings, identify device, restart & shut down |
| `Network` | Name & address of the station, Wi-Fi networks, emergency hotspot |
| `Security` | Password for the settings |
| `Analysis` | Start or pause bird detection, detected animal groups, fine-tuning, error log |

### Protecting the settings with a password

Without a password, anyone who can open the web interface can also change the settings.
Under `Security`, use `Enable protection` to set a password with at least eight characters.
From then on, the station asks for the password for every change. Overview, detections and statistics stay visible to everyone.

Remember the password well. Without it, the settings can no longer be changed.

### Presets

A preset determines which pages and functions are visible. You choose it under `General` → `Preset`:

- **Schoolyard birds (full):** all pages, with a link to information about each species. This is the default.
- **Research yourself:** as above, but without the link to species information, so people look things up themselves.
- **Display only / projector:** only the overview in large type, without menu or buttons.

The settings stay reachable with every preset if you open the address directly: `http://<device-name>.local:8080/#/settings`.
A preset is not a protection. That's what the password is for.

### Time without internet

The Raspberry Pi has no built-in clock. Without internet, the station takes the time from your phone or laptop during setup.
If the time is wrong later, for example after being without power for a while, tap `Set time from this device` under `General` → `Time & date`.

## As an app on the home screen

You can add the web interface to your home screen from your browser menu. It then opens like an app.
This only works while your device is on the same network as the station; without a connection to the station, the app shows nothing.
