## Checklist: What do I need?

<Checklist id="project02-components">

- A **Raspberry Pi**
  - **Raspberry Pi 5** approx. $50–140 or **Raspberry Pi 4B** approx. $40–80 – recommended if many people open the web interface at the same time
  - **Raspberry Pi 3B/3B+** approx. $40
  - **Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W** approx. $20 – the smallest supported model. A good fit for a station that runs on its own outdoors and is only checked now and then.
  - The Raspberry Pi 1, Pi Zero and Pi Zero W are not suitable. Their processor is too old for Smartfinch.
- A **microSD card** with at least 16 GB
  - The card holds the system, the detections and the audio recordings. The larger the card, the more recordings are kept. When space runs low, Smartfinch deletes the oldest recordings by itself; the detections stay.
  - For the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W and the Pi 3, a fast, high-quality card pays off, because these models use part of the card as extra memory.
- possibly an **adapter or card reader** to connect the microSD card to your computer
- A **power supply** that fits your Raspberry Pi
- A **USB microphone** or a **USB sound card with a microphone**
- For the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, also a **micro-USB OTG adapter** to connect the USB microphone
- A **computer** with internet access to download the image and write it to the SD card
- A **phone, tablet or laptop with Wi-Fi** to set up the station and view the results
- Optional: an **enclosure** or a sheltered spot if the station will stand outdoors (see [Placing the station](../assembly/))

</Checklist>

You don't need a screen, keyboard or mouse. The station is set up entirely in the browser.
